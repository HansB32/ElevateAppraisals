Client Reference Guide

Reference guide for private-use clients covering appraisal process standards, report formats, and preparation requirements.

Private Use:Estate, divorce, tax, pre-listing/market value, and advisory assignments.

Lenders/AMCs:Purchase, refinance, HELOC, and portfolio work via standard engagement workflow.

Private Use:This guide provides general context and does not replace engagement terms.

Lenders/AMCs:Rely on engagement letters and ordering portal requirements for assignment-specific scope and timing.

Why you might need an appraisal

Appraisals provide a documented, independent opinion of market value when decisions carry legal or financial weight. Common reasons include estate settlement, divorce support, tax appeals, pre-listing decisions, or private lending and family transfers.

What appraisal you might need

Single-family

A full appraisal includes interior and exterior inspection and is the most common option for a complete value opinion. Exterior-only or desktop options may be appropriate when access is limited or the assignment is advisory rather than lending.

Multifamily (2–4 units)

Multifamily appraisals follow the same core process, with added rent and income analysis. A full appraisal is typical; exterior-only or desktop options may be considered for limited-access or advisory needs.

Land

Land appraisals include site inspection; This appraisal is specifically vacant residential use properties. If the land is not vacant or appraisal is for construction to permanent, a different type of appraisal may be required.

Rent schedule

A rent schedule provides a market rent opinion for single-family rentals. It can be paired with a value appraisal or ordered on its own for rental decision support. This can be completed with long-term or short-term rental data.

How it is typically completed

Most residential appraisals rely on the Sales Comparison Approach. The appraiser inspects the property as required, selects recent comparable sales, and makes market-supported adjustments for differences. The report reconciles those comparisons into a single value opinion with photos, maps, and written support.
