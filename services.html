Residential Real Estate Appraisals Clear valuation for residential real estate decisions.

Why clients request this

  • Market positioning for listing, purchase, or FSBO negotiations
  • Estate, trust, and tax planning with reliable date-of-death values
  • Family law and partnership settlements needing impartial support
  • Refinance, HELOC, or PMI removal based on current equity

Typical assignments & deliverables

  • Single-family homes, custom builds, and high-end residences
  • Condominiums, townhomes, and PUD units
  • 2–4 unit income properties with rent schedule analysis
  • Unique rural cabins and recreational retreats
  • PDF narrative report with comparable grid, photo log, and market exhibits

Use cases

  • Private Use: estate, divorce, tax, pre-listing/market value, and advisory assignments.
  • Lenders/AMCs: purchase, refinance, HELOC, and portfolio work via standard engagement workflow.
Residential Land & Site Appraisals Valuations for homesites, short plats, and acreage slated for residential use.

Why clients request this

  • Homesite acquisition or pre-construction budgeting
  • Lot releases, boundary line adjustments, and feasibility checks
  • Estate gifting, charitable contributions, or tax planning
  • Verification of contributory value for excess residential land

Typical assignments & deliverables

  • Vacant infill lots and tear-down sites
  • Rural acreage, view parcels, and hobby-farm homesites
  • Minor subdivisions and clustered residential concepts
  • Residential site-work progress inspections
  • Land grid with mapped comparable sales and narrative commentary

Use cases

  • Private Use: estate, tax, advisory, and pre-development planning for residential sites.
  • Lenders/AMCs: residential land collateral for purchase or portfolio assignments via engagement workflow.
Investment & Income-Producing Properties Decision-ready analysis for small residential portfolios and hybrid owners.

Why clients request this

  • Underwriting for DSCR, portfolio, or private placement loans
  • Hold/sell decisions and quarterly portfolio check-ins
  • Rental market studies to support lease renewals or repositioning
  • Partner buy-in/out, recapitalization, or succession planning

Typical assignments & deliverables

  • Form 1004 or 1025 market value opinions for 1–4 unit rentals
  • Form 1073 condominium investor reports
  • Form 1007 rent schedules with market-supported adjustments
  • Operating statement review with income capitalization
  • Rent roll cross-check, sensitivity tables, and PDF appraisal report

Note: For duplexes through fourplexes we use Form 1025 when unit-level income data is required; it includes rent and income reconciliation with the value conclusion.

Use cases

  • Private Use: advisory, portfolio review, and partnership planning for small residential income assets.
  • Lenders/AMCs: DSCR, purchase, refinance, and portfolio assignments via engagement workflow.
