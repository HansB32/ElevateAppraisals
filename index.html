How It Works

A streamlined four-step process tailored to residential lending, estate, divorce, and private-use assignments.

1

Submit Request

Share the property address, intended use, access information, and target delivery date so we can confirm scope and fee.

2

Market Analysis

We pair on-site observations with MLS data, public records, and local trend insight to select the strongest comparable sales or rentals.

3

Appraisal Report

You receive a clear narrative report with photo exhibits, maps, reconciled value and/or rent conclusions, and supporting commentary.

4

Client Support

We remain available for clarification, lender or attorney follow-up, and supplemental reporting throughout your process.
